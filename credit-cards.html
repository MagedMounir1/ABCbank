Common questions

How long does a replacement card take?

Replacement cards are dispatched within one working day and typically arrive in three to five days. We'll call you once it's on its way.

What happens when I dispute a transaction?

We raise a case with our fraud team the moment you report it. Most disputes are resolved within ten working days, and we contact you with the result.

Can I increase my credit limit?

Yes. Limit reviews are available after six months of account history. Speak to us and we'll talk through your options.

Are there charges for using my card abroad?

Platinum, Premier and VIP cards carry no foreign transaction fee. Gold cards carry a small percentage charge on non-IQD spending.
